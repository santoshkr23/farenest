Privacy policy

Last updated 30 October 2026

FareNest ("we") runs the FareNest website and mobile apps, which compare travel prices from partner sites. This policy explains what we collect.

What we collect

  • Search details you enter (airports, cities, dates, number of travellers) so we can fetch prices. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number or payment details.
  • Click records: when you choose a deal, we log the partner, the item, the price shown, the time, your IP address and browser or device type, so travel partners can credit us for referrals.
  • On your device: the app stores your recent and saved searches locally. They are not sent to us. Deleting the app removes them.

How we use it

To show prices, to measure which partners people choose, to receive affiliate commissions, and to keep the service working and secure. We do not sell personal data and do not use it for advertising profiles.

Travel partners

When you open a deal you go to the partner's own site or app. Bookings and payments happen there and are covered by that partner's privacy policy, not this one.

Retention

Click records are kept for up to 24 months for commission reconciliation, then deleted.

Your rights

You can ask us to access or delete data linked to you, subject to applicable law including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. Contact us at the address on the support page.

Children

FareNest is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We will post updates on this page and change the date above.
